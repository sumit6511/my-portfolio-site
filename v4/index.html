About

A Computer Science student who is happiest building complete things — the model, the API and the interface.

I'm studying B.Sc. CSIT at St. Xavier's College in Kathmandu, and I'm passionate about software development, machine learning and technology that's actually useful. I like projects that push my boundaries and teach me something new.

Lately that has meant full-stack applications — React on the front, Node.js, Fastify or FastAPI behind it, MongoDB or PostgreSQL underneath — and machine-learning work like natural-language processing and retrieval-augmented generation.

When I'm not coding, I'm exploring new technologies, reading tech blogs, contributing to open-source projects, or out on a trail.
